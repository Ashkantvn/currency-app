# Overview
This project focuses on designing a cryptocurrency market dashboard that allows users to explore digital assets, understand market trends, and analyze cryptocurrency data in one place.

The platform brings together essential market information, including cryptocurrency prices, market trends, historical data, and detailed coin information. It presents this data through a clear and organized interface, helping users explore market conditions, compare digital assets, and better understand market performance.

The goal is to make cryptocurrency market data more accessible and understandable, providing both beginners and casual crypto users with an intuitive environment to explore and analyze market information.

# Problem Statement
The cryptocurrency market contains thousands of digital assets and a large volume of constantly changing information. Finding, understanding, and comparing relevant market data can be challenging, especially when information is scattered across multiple platforms.

Users may struggle to get a clear overview of market conditions, analyze cryptocurrency performance, compare different assets, and interpret various market indicators.

For beginners and casual users, the complexity of financial data and unfamiliar terminology can make understanding the cryptocurrency market even more difficult.

This project addresses these challenges by exploring how cryptocurrency market data can be collected, organized, and presented through an intuitive dashboard that simplifies data exploration, comparison, and market analysis.

# Target Audience

The target audience for this cryptocurrency market dashboard consists of individuals who want to explore cryptocurrency data, understand market conditions, and access market information through a simple and organized interface.

The platform primarily focuses on beginners and casual cryptocurrency users, while also considering individuals who have a general interest in the cryptocurrency market.

## 1. Primary Audience

### Crypto Beginners

Individuals who are new to the cryptocurrency market and have limited knowledge of digital assets and market indicators.

**Characteristics:**
- Limited experience with cryptocurrency.
- Basic or little knowledge of market terminology.
- Interested in learning about digital assets and market conditions.
- Prefer simple and easy-to-understand information.

### Casual Crypto Users

Individuals who have a basic understanding of cryptocurrency and occasionally or regularly explore market information.

**Characteristics:**
- Familiar with basic cryptocurrency concepts.
- Interested in following market conditions and cryptocurrency performance.
- Prefer accessing market data from a single platform.
- Want to explore and compare digital assets without using complex analytical tools.

## 2. Secondary Audience

### Crypto Enthusiasts

Individuals who have a general interest in cryptocurrencies and enjoy exploring digital assets and market-related information.

**Characteristics:**
- Interested in the cryptocurrency market and its developments.
- Enjoy exploring different digital assets.
- Interested in market trends and cryptocurrency performance.
- Want convenient access to a variety of market information.
